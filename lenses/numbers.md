# Shape: a number, a time or a count that is slightly wrong

Money, percentages, counts, limits, ranks, dates and durations. The shape is arithmetic or comparison that is right in the example the author had in mind and wrong at the edge: rounding that creates or loses a cent, a total that doesn't equal its parts, an off-by-one at a boundary, a tie nobody decided, zero, negative and very large values, a text compared as a number or the other way round, a missing value treated as zero, a time zone or daylight-saving change, a clock that differs between browser and server, units mixed up, a page of results that skips or repeats a row.

Work real numbers through the code by hand, especially the awkward ones. State the input and the wrong output.
